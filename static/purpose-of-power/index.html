The Purpose of the Power

How a Democratic Congress should use the majority voters gave it

Andover Democratic Town Committee·Version 1.0·November 2026

The November 2026 elections gave Democrats control of both chambers of Congress, and advice about what to do with that power arrived from every direction. Some urged the new majority to do as little as possible beyond the cost of living. Others urged it to do everything at once.

This paper argues for a third course: accept the power and use it with discipline. The test for every hearing, subpoena, and bill should be how much it matters, not how many wins it produces or how easily they come. The paper names where that work should begin, and it ends on the reason for doing it at all, which is to restore a government of the people, by the people, and for the people.
